Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Toon Studio is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the L7moni YouTube channel, for that channel. This policy explains what it does with data from YouTube and Google.

YouTube API Services

Toon Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • The signed-in channel's basic details (channel name and ID).
  • The channel's own uploaded videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comment counts).
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports for its videos (for example views per day, average share watched and audience retention).

It does not access any other channel's private data, and it does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated, anonymous analytics YouTube provides to the channel owner.

How the data is used

  • To upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags and cover.
  • To post one comment from the channel under each of its own videos.
  • To show the channel owner how each video performs.

How it is stored and shared

The sign-in token and the analytics numbers are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with or sent to anyone else. Analytics numbers are refreshed daily and kept no longer than the owner keeps the tool; they are deleted when the tool is removed, and at the latest 30 days after the owner stops using it.

Revoking access

You can remove Toon Studio's access to your Google account at any time from Google's third-party connections page (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Once revoked, the tool can no longer read or post anything.

Cookies

This website and Toon Studio do not use cookies or tracking.
